# Corrections for the image AI

The 8 images are beautiful, but they show a **luxury resort villa**, not this house. They are too big, too glassy, too grand, and they don't match each other. Images 1 and 4 even show two different houses.

Paste **section 1** at the top of every request, then the correction for that image.

---

## 1. The rules for every image

```
This is ONE real family house in Uganda, on a normal suburban plot, built in concrete block with smooth render.
Every image must show the same house. Keep it realistic and modest, not a luxury villa or resort.

THE HOUSE
- Two storeys. FLAT roof hidden behind a low white parapet with a thin BLACK coping along the top. No overhanging roof slabs.
- Simple box shape. Plain white rendered walls. No stone cladding, no timber cladding, no marble.
- Ordinary punched windows (single or double panels), not glass curtain walls. Black frames.
- Raised base (plinth) about 75 cm high, in grey, with 5 broad grey steps up to the front.
- Full width covered front veranda. Above it, a full width front balcony, carried on 4 slim square white columns.
- One full height grey panel framing the front door, edged with thin black lines.
- A black water tank on a small white stand on the roof, at the back.
- White boundary wall with black coping and a black sliding gate. Grass, a few local trees, a paved driveway. No palm avenues, no resort planting.

COLOURS
- About 70% white, 20% grey, 10% black. Black only for frames, railings, lights, doors and small details.

RAILINGS
- Black, VERTICAL bars, gaps under 10 cm, black top rail, about 1.05 m high.
- Never horizontal bars or cables (a child can climb them). Never glass.

INSIDE
- White walls, light grey floor tiles. No marble floors, no fireplaces, no chandeliers made of crystal.
- Furniture is grey and white with black tables. Comfortable and normal sized, not showroom sized.

CAMERA
- Eye level, about 1.6 m high, realistic lens (24 to 35 mm). No ultra wide distortion that makes rooms look huge.
- Daylight or early evening. Soft, natural light.
```

## 2. Image by image

**What is wrong**
- The left block is a two storey glass curtain wall. The real living hall has normal windows plus a row of high windows near the top.
- The balcony is a deep box with a heavy overhanging frame. The real balcony is a simple slab across the full front, on 4 slim columns.
- The railing has horizontal bars. It must have vertical bars.
- Palm trees and resort lighting make it look like a hotel.
- No raised grey base, no grey entrance panel, no parapet, no water tank, no boundary wall.

**Tell the AI**
```
Redo the front view. Same house as the rules. Straight-on view from the gate at eye level, early evening.
Full width covered veranda on a grey plinth with 5 broad grey steps. Full width balcony above it on 4 slim white columns, black VERTICAL-bar railing.

Flat roof behind a white parapet with a thin black coping. Black water tank visible at the back.
Small black wall lights beside the doors. Grass, two local trees, white boundary wall, black gate. No palms, no glass curtain wall.
```

- The sofa ring, marble floor and full height windows are all exaggerated.

