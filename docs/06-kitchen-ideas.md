# Kitchen: seven finish directions

Seven visual options for the same compact kitchen. Each keeps the L-shaped cabinet run, rear sink window, island, two stools and half-height opening to the living hall in the same positions. The camera and room scale stay consistent; only cabinet finishes and details change.

## Shared design rules

- Keep the existing kitchen footprint and circulation clear between the island and the L-shaped counters.
- Keep the sink under the existing rear window, with the cooking run and refrigerator in their current positions.
- Use the house palette: white first, then charcoal/black and soft grey. Avoid wood, beige, gold, and marble or stone looks.
- Use matte cabinet fronts, simple black hardware, practical drawers and a durable plain white solid-surface counter.
- Keep the half-height separation to the living hall and the visible stair/gallery relationship as shown in the existing photo.

## Practical checks before building

- Confirm appliance sizes, door swings, refrigerator clearance and the cooking ventilation route against the plan.
- Keep clear working space around the island and two stools; confirm exact dimensions on site before fixing the island footprint.
- Coordinate sockets, extractor ducting, under-cabinet lights, water and drainage before closing walls.
- These are visual studies, not construction drawings. Confirm dimensions, materials and installation details with the designer or contractor.
