# TV wall: six ideas

The back wall of the double height living hall, with:
- two mouldings running side by side from floor to ceiling,
- a 100 inch TV between them,
- a piece of furniture below the TV,
- mostly white, balanced with black.

**A shared rule for the vertical features:** they should look as if they **grow out of the ceiling cornice**, the moulding where the walls meet the ceiling. Use the same white and let the cornice meet each feature cleanly, so the wall and ceiling read as one design.

---

## Shared TV and furniture sizes

| Item | Size |
|---|---|
| 100 inch TV | about 2.21 m wide × 1.25 m tall |
| Clear space between the two mouldings | 3.0 to 3.2 m, so the TV has about 40 cm of air on each side |
| Centre of the TV above the floor | 1.4 to 1.5 m. The sofa is about 5 m away, so this keeps the screen comfortable to watch. |
| Furniture under the TV | top at about 45 to 50 cm, 2.8 to 3.8 m long |
| Wall height | about 6.5 m, the full double height |

**Window arrangement:** the TV wall is kept solid, with no windows. High windows on the side walls bring daylight into the room. Confirm this against the latest architectural plan before construction.

---

## Idea 1: Twin pilasters, a quiet classic

**The look:** two flat white pilasters, square columns built against the wall, each about 36 cm wide. Each has a recessed panel down its face with a single **thin black line inlaid** in the middle. At the top, each pilaster widens into a simple square cap that runs straight into the ceiling cornice, as if holding it up.

**Between them:**
- the TV, set flush,
- above it, two nested picture frame mouldings rising toward the ceiling, which make the tall wall feel finished,
- below it, a long black floating console, about 2.8 m, fixed to the wall with no legs.

**Black balance:** the inlay lines, the console, the TV, and two small black picture lights over the frames.

**Tie to the ceiling:** the cornice breaks forward around each pilaster cap and steps back in between.

## Idea 2: The panel ladder, modern and graphic

**The look:** two **slim** white mouldings, only about 12 cm wide, running floor to ceiling like the rails of a ladder. Between them, a stack of white rectangular frame mouldings climbs the wall, evenly spaced, three of them above the TV.

**Between them:**
- the TV sits inside a **charcoal** frame panel about 2.6 × 1.65 m, so the screen disappears into it when switched off,
- below it, a low black bench, about 3 m long and 35 cm high, which also holds the speaker and decoder.

**Black balance:** the charcoal frame and the black bench carry all the weight low down. Everything above is white line work.

**Tie to the ceiling:** the top frame of the ladder is spaced so its upper edge lines up with the bottom of the cornice. The two rails die into the cornice with the same profile.

## Idea 3: Fluted bands with a black shadow line, the boldest

**The look:** two wide **fluted** bands, vertical ribs about 60 cm wide in white, from floor to ceiling. Along the inner edge of each band runs a **20 mm black shadow gap**, a narrow recessed line. A hidden warm LED strip washes light down the ribs at night.

**Between them:**
- smooth white plaster and the TV, floated on the wall with nothing around it,
- a **very long** low black bench, about 3.8 m, with a white stone top. It runs past the two bands and ties them together.

**Black balance:** two crisp vertical black lines, one long black bench, and the TV. Very little black, but every piece is strong.

**Tie to the ceiling:** instead of a traditional cornice, the black shadow gap **turns at the ceiling and runs around the whole room** as a recessed line. The fluted bands and the ceiling then share one continuous black line.

```

## Idea 4: The tall arched niche, the most dramatic

**The look:** the two mouldings rise from the floor and **join overhead in a gentle arch** just below the ceiling, framing one tall niche the full height of the double height wall. The moulding is about 30 cm wide, in white. The back of the niche is **deep charcoal**, smooth plaster or microcement.

**Between them:**
- the TV against the charcoal back. When it is off, it almost vanishes, and the arch becomes the feature.
- below it, a black built in cabinet filling the full width of the niche, with four fluted doors.

**Black balance:** the charcoal niche is the one bold dark shape on an all white wall. The white arch moulding outlines it.

**Tie to the ceiling:** a short white **keystone** drops from the cornice to the top of the arch, so the arch hangs from the ceiling moulding.

```

## Which to choose

| Idea | Feel | Effort to build | Black amount |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Twin pilasters | Classic, calm | Medium, carpentry and plaster | Low |
| 2 Panel ladder | Modern, graphic | Easy, stock moulding strips | Medium, low down |
| 3 Fluted bands | Bold, minimal | Medium, fluted panels and an LED channel | Low, very crisp |
| 4 Arched niche | Dramatic, signature | Hardest, a curved moulding | High, one dark shape |

**For a white, grey and black house:** Idea 3 fits best, because its black ceiling line ties the whole room together. Idea 4 makes the strongest single statement.

**Plan the hidden parts early, before plastering:**
- a recessed box behind the TV for the power and HDMI sockets,
- a 50 mm conduit down to the console for the cables,
- for Idea 3, the LED channel.

## Two more minimal ideas (added 7 October 2026)

Both use the same room and camera as the first four designs. The TV wall has no windows. The wall above the TV stays plain: no lights, frames, shelves, artwork or decoration.

### Idea 5: Shadow line pilasters
- **Pilasters:** two plain white pilasters, about 36 cm wide, from floor to ceiling, with simple caps joining the white cornice.
- **Black detail:** one fine black inlay runs down the centre of each pilaster.
- **Wall:** smooth white plaster between the pilasters, completely blank above the screen.
- **TV and furniture:** centred TV, with one long low black floating console below.
- **Image:** `images/tv-wall/05-shadow-line-pilasters.webp`

### Idea 6: Grey slab and fluted columns
- **Columns:** two white fluted columns run from floor to ceiling and join the cornice.
- **Slab:** one smooth pale grey plaster panel fills the bay between them.
- **TV:** centred on the panel, with an empty stretch of grey wall above.
- **Bench:** one long low black bench beneath the TV.
- **Image:** `images/tv-wall/06-grey-slab-and-fluted-columns.webp`

Both images use the same family room, window placement and camera as the other TV wall images.
