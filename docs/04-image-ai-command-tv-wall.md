# Command for the image AI: first four TV wall designs

Paste the block below as one message. If the tool makes one image at a time, send everything above "IMAGE 1" each time, followed by the block for the image you want.

For minimalist options 5 and 6, see `03-tv-wall-ideas.md`. If it makes the room too grand, reply: "Make the room smaller and plainer. It is a family house, not a hotel. Keep the same TV wall design."

```
TASK
Create 4 images. Each shows one design idea for the same TV wall in my living room.
The 4 images must show the same room from the same camera position, so I can compare them side by side. Only the TV wall design changes.

THE ROOM (same in all 4 images)
- A real family house in Uganda, concrete block with smooth plaster. Realistic and modest, not a luxury hotel or villa.
- A double height living room, about 7.5 m wide and 9 m deep, ceiling about 6.5 m high.
- The TV wall is the back wall, about 7.5 m wide and 6.5 m high, painted white.
- A white crown cornice (ceiling moulding) runs around the top of every wall.
- A row of high windows near the top of the side walls brings in daylight.
- An upstairs gallery runs along the front of the room at about 3.5 m high, with a black vertical-bar railing. Show only its edge.
- Light grey floor tiles. A grey L-shaped sofa faces the TV about 5 m away, with a black coffee table and a grey rug.
- Colours: about 70% white, 20% grey, 10% black. No wood, beige, gold, marble, stone or fireplace.

CAMERA (same in all 4 images)
- Standing behind the sofa at eye level, about 1.6 m high, looking straight at the TV wall.
- 28 mm lens, no fisheye or ultra wide distortion. Show the whole wall from floor to ceiling.
- Soft natural daylight. Photorealistic interior photograph.

RULES FOR EVERY DESIGN
- Two mouldings run side by side from the floor to the ceiling, about 3.1 m apart.
- They must look as if they grow out of the ceiling cornice: same white, same profile style, joined into it.
- A 100 inch TV (2.21 m wide, 1.25 m tall) between the mouldings, its centre about 1.45 m above the floor.
- Black furniture below the TV. Mainly white, balanced with black.

IMAGE 1: TWIN PILASTERS
- Two flat white pilasters 36 cm wide, floor to ceiling, each with a recessed panel and one thin black line inlaid down the centre.
- A simple square cap at the top of each joins the cornice, which steps forward around it.
- Two nested white picture-frame mouldings above the TV. A long black floating console, 2.8 m, below. Two small black picture lights.

IMAGE 2: PANEL LADDER
- Two slim white mouldings 12 cm wide, floor to ceiling, like ladder rails.
- Three evenly spaced white frame mouldings stack above the TV; the top frame lines up with the cornice.
- The TV sits in a matte charcoal panel 2.6 m by 1.65 m. A low black bench, 3 m long and 35 cm high, below.

IMAGE 3: FLUTED BANDS
- Two white fluted (reeded) bands 60 cm wide, floor to ceiling.
- A thin black 20 mm shadow gap along the inner edge of each band; the same black line runs around the ceiling edge instead of a cornice.
- Warm hidden LED light down the flutes. Smooth white wall and a floating TV between. A long low black bench, 3.8 m, with a white stone top, running past both bands.

IMAGE 4: TALL ARCHED NICHE
- A white moulding 30 cm wide rises on both sides and joins overhead in a gentle arch just below the ceiling, forming one niche 3.1 m wide.
- Smooth deep charcoal plaster inside the niche. A short white keystone links the arch to the cornice.
- The TV against the charcoal. A black built-in cabinet with four fluted doors fills the bottom of the niche.

AVOID IN ALL 4
- A room bigger than described, extra floors or a second gallery.
- Chandeliers, fireplaces, stone or marble walls, wood floors, glass or horizontal-bar railings.
- Text, labels or logos. Clutter: at most one plant and two decorative objects.

OUTPUT
- 4 separate landscape images, 16:9, high resolution, named TV-wall-1-twin-pilasters, TV-wall-2-panel-ladder, TV-wall-3-fluted-bands, TV-wall-4-arched-niche.
- Check each before sending: two mouldings floor to ceiling joined to the cornice, 100 inch TV centred, black furniture below, same room and camera as the others.
```
